Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2025

Introduction

Snippd is a Chrome extension that lets you capture screenshots of web pages — the visible viewport, a full scrollable page, or a selected region — and annotate them in a built-in editor before copying or downloading the result.

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what Snippd does and does not do with your information. In short: Snippd collects no personal data, sends nothing to external servers, and keeps all screenshot processing on your device.

Data Collection

Snippd collects zero user data. We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or share any personal information, browsing history, account details, or usage analytics.

  • No data is transmitted to any server operated by Snippd or any third party.
  • No cookies are set by Snippd.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising tools are used.
  • All screenshot capture, editing, and export happens locally in your browser.

When you take a screenshot, the image is stored temporarily in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) so you can access recent captures in the editor's history panel. Up to 50 recent captures are kept until you delete them. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear your browser's extension data.

Permissions Explanation

Snippd requests only the permissions needed to capture and edit screenshots when you explicitly start a capture. None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit personal data.

  • activeTab Allows Snippd to access the tab you are currently viewing only when you start a capture — by clicking the toolbar icon, using a keyboard shortcut, or choosing an option from the right-click menu. Snippd does not access tabs in the background without your action.
  • tabs Used to identify which tab and window to capture, take the screenshot itself, send coordination messages during full-page and region capture, open the editor after a capture, and show optional capture progress on the extension badge. Snippd does not read or record the content of your tabs beyond what is needed for the capture you requested.
  • scripting Used to inject Snippd's content script into the active tab when needed — for example, to scroll the page during a full-page capture, display the region-selection overlay, show status messages, and restore the page afterward. Injection only happens on tabs where you have started a capture.
  • contextMenus Used to add a "Snippd" entry to the right-click menu on web pages, giving you quick access to Print Page, Scroll & Capture, and Snip Area without opening the toolbar popup.
  • clipboardWrite Used in the editor so you can copy your finished screenshot to the clipboard when you choose to. Snippd only writes the image you explicitly copy; it does not read your clipboard.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) Required because users may want to capture screenshots on any website they visit. Snippd needs access to the page being captured to scroll it for full-page screenshots, display the region-selection overlay, and coordinate capture on sites across the web. Snippd does not collect, transmit, or store data from the pages you visit.

Third Parties

Snippd does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. No data is shared with third parties because no data is collected in the first place.

The Chrome Web Store is operated by Google. Installing or updating Snippd through the store is subject to Google's own privacy policy, which is separate from this one.
