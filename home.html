Full page screenshot & screen capture for Chrome

PageDrop is a free Chrome extension that captures visible tabs, entire scrollable pages, or any region. Annotate, crop, blur sensitive details, and export as PNG, JPEG, or PDF — all with privacy-first activeTab access only.

Add to Chrome — Free Privacy policy

Available on the Chrome Web Store.

Tip: After installing, click the puzzle piece (Extensions) in the Chrome toolbar, then click the pin next to PageDrop so it stays visible for one-click access.

Capture modes

  • Visible tab (Print Page) — screenshot what’s on screen right now
  • Full page (Scroll & Capture) — automatic scrolling capture of long pages with progress indicator
  • Region (Snip Area) — drag to snip any area of the page

Built-in editor

  • Opens in a browser tab so you can review before saving
  • Draw, shapes, arrows, and text annotations
  • Crop to focus on what matters
  • Blur / redact — permanently pixelate emails, names, or IDs before sharing
  • Export as PNG, JPEG, or PDF
  • Multi-page PDF when a very long page is split into parts
  • Recent captures history in the session — switch, continue editing, or remove
  • Copy to clipboard, undo, rotate, light/dark theme

Privacy-first

  • No broad <all_urls> permission — activeTab only
  • Content script runs only when you start a capture
  • Screenshots stay local until you copy or download
  • No cloud upload during normal use

Keyboard shortcuts

Customize shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Windows & Linux

  • Alt+Shift+V — Print Page (visible tab screenshot)
  • Alt+Shift+F — Scroll & Capture (full page)
  • Alt+Shift+S — Snip Area (region)

Mac

  • ⌥⇧V (Option + Shift + V) — Print Page
  • ⌥⇧F (Option + Shift + F) — Scroll & Capture
  • ⌥⇧S (Option + Shift + S) — Snip Area

Frequently asked questions

What is PageDrop?

PageDrop is a free Chrome extension for capturing visible tabs, full scrollable web pages, or custom screen regions. Every capture opens in a built-in editor where you can annotate, crop, blur sensitive details, and export — all processed locally in your browser.

How do I take a full-page screenshot in Chrome?

Install PageDrop, open any normal website, then click the toolbar icon and choose Scroll & Capture, or press Alt+Shift+F (Option+Shift+F on Mac). PageDrop scrolls automatically and stitches the page into one image.

Is PageDrop safe and private?

Yes. PageDrop uses activeTab permission only — no broad site access at install. Screenshots are processed locally and are not uploaded to external servers during normal use.

Can PageDrop blur or redact sensitive information?

Yes. Drag the blur/redact tool over any area to permanently pixelate emails, names, IDs, or other sensitive content before sharing or exporting.

What file formats does PageDrop export?

PNG (lossless), JPEG (smaller files), and PDF. When a very long page is split into multiple images, you can combine all parts into one multi-page PDF or export the current part only.

What pages cannot be captured?

PageDrop cannot capture chrome:// pages, the Chrome Web Store, or the New Tab page. Open a normal website first. Refresh the page once (F5) after installing or updating.
